Visual Regression, With a Second Pair of Eyes

Screenshots in, flagged issues and formal bug reports out. You still confirm what's real.

By RN ~4 min read Series: AI-Augmented QA
Manual, No AI Manual + AI Automated, Playwright AI QA Agent

Take screenshots, hand them to Claude, get a second pair of eyes on what's visually wrong, then write up the findings as proper bug reports.

What you actually do

  • Take screenshots of the UI at peakandpack-ui.onrender.com
  • Share them with Claude and ask it to identify visual issues
  • Ask Claude to generate a more thorough checklist based on the Phase 1 requirements doc
  • Use Claude to write up a formal visual bug report for anything found

Prompts that actually work

Here is a screenshot of the PeakAndPack product page. What visual issues do you see?

Generate a visual testing checklist for an e-commerce product listing page covering layout, typography, spacing, and data display.

Write a visual bug report for: product card shows negative price -$89.00

Claude sees what you stop noticing. After looking at the same product grid for an hour, a slightly misaligned price label stops registering. A fresh screenshot review catches small layout drift that familiarity has made invisible to you.

Your deliverable

One file: visual-testing-ai-assisted.md, combining your screenshot review with Claude's flagged issues and a written bug report for each confirmed finding.

Time: ~45 minutes. Faster than the manual pass, mostly because writing up formal bug reports is the part Claude accelerates most.
