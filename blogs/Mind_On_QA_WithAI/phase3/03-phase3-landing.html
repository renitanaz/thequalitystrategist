Test Case Creation: Turning Strategy Into Steps

The strategy said what matters. Now it becomes specific steps anyone could follow and check.

By RN ~4 min read Series: AI-Augmented QA

Strategy decided what matters. Test cases turn that into something you can actually execute: specific steps, specific inputs, specific expected results.

40-60test cases, written by hand
6feature areas to cover
3case types: happy, negative, edge

Three kinds of test case, every time

Happy path
Valid input, expected success. "User adds a tent to cart, cart total updates correctly."
Negative
Invalid input, expected failure. "User registers with no password, gets a 400, not a silent success."
Edge case
Boundary values, empty fields, weird input. "Search with zero-length query, search with emoji, price at exactly $0.00."
All three, every feature
Products, Auth, Cart, Checkout, Orders, Search each need all three types covered, not just the happy path.

Four ways to get there

Volume isn't the goal. 60 test cases that all test the happy path are worth less than 20 that cover happy, negative, and edge cases across the riskiest areas identified in Phase 2.
