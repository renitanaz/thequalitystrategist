About Grove & Bean

Our story

Grove & Bean started in 2019 as a single roaster in a shared kitchen space. Today we work with three family farms directly, roasting everything to order out of our own space.

What we believe

Coffee tastes best close to its roast date. Everything about how we run the roastery, small batches, direct relationships, shipping fast, comes back to that one idea.
