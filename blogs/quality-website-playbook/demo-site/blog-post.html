Why roast date matters more than origin

Everyone asks where our beans are from. Fewer people ask when they were roasted, and that's the number that actually predicts how the cup tastes.

Freshness fades faster than people expect

Coffee starts losing its best aromatics within two to three weeks of roasting. A bag can look identical on a shelf at week one and week six, but the cup will taste noticeably flatter by the second half of that window.

What we do differently

We roast in batches sized to what we'll actually ship that week, so nothing sits waiting for an order. It's a smaller margin for error on our end, and a better cup on yours.
