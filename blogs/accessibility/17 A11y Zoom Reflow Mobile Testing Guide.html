17 A11y Zoom Reflow Mobile Testing Guide

How to test zoom, reflow and reduced motion on iOS and Android. Covers pinch-to-zoom, text size scaling, font size settings, and the Reduce Motion system preference.

⚠️ Physical device required. Mobile emulation in DevTools does not replicate pinch-to-zoom behaviour or OS-level reduced motion settings accurately.
iOS · Safari

Testing on iPhone and iPad

Settings to configure before testing:
  • Reduce Motion: Settings, Accessibility, Motion, Reduce Motion, Toggle ON
  • Larger Text: Settings, Accessibility, Display and Text Size, Larger Text, drag slider to largest
  • Zoom: Settings, Accessibility, Zoom, Toggle ON (enables full-screen or window zoom)
1
Pinch-to-zoom disabled
1
Open the site in Safari. Attempt to pinch-to-zoom the page.
2
If nothing happens, inspect the page source for user-scalable=no or maximum-scale=1 in the viewport meta tag.
Failure: Pinch-to-zoom does not work. Viewport meta tag contains user-scalable=no or maximum-scale=1. WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
Pass: Page zooms freely on pinch. Content reflows and remains readable. No content is lost.
2
Content breaks at large text size
1
Settings, Accessibility, Display and Text Size, Larger Text. Drag slider to the largest position.
2
Open the site in Safari. Navigate through each key page and interactive component.
3
Check: does text overflow containers, overlap other elements, or disappear?
Failure: Button labels truncate. Card text overflows its container. Nav items disappear. Fixed-height containers clip text.
Pass: All text remains readable and all interactive elements usable at the largest text size.
3
Animations not reduced under Reduce Motion
1
Settings, Accessibility, Motion, Reduce Motion, Toggle ON.
2
Open the site in Safari. Navigate between pages. Observe transitions, hover effects, loading spinners, and carousels.
3
Note any animation that still plays at full intensity.
Failure: Page transitions still slide or fade. Carousels still auto-advance with smooth animation. Skeleton loaders still shimmer. The site is not respecting prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.
Pass: Transitions become instant or fade only. Spinners slow significantly. All content still accessible and functional.
4
Reflow check at native iOS viewport
1
Open the site in Safari on iPhone (portrait mode).
2
Scroll through each key page without zooming. Check for horizontal scroll at default zoom.
3
Then pinch-to-zoom to approximately 200%. Scroll through again. Check that all content remains reachable vertically.
Pass: No horizontal scroll at default zoom. At 200% zoom, all content reachable by vertical scroll. Navigation accessible.
Failure: Horizontal scroll bar appears at default or zoomed view. Nav items disappear at zoom. Modal unreachable.
Android · Chrome

Testing on Android

Settings to configure before testing:
  • Remove Animations: Settings, Accessibility, Remove Animations, Toggle ON
  • Font Size: Settings, Accessibility, Font Size, drag to largest setting
  • Display Size: Settings, Accessibility, Display Size, increase to largest
1
Pinch-to-zoom disabled in Chrome
1
Open the site in Chrome. Attempt to pinch-to-zoom.
2
If zoom does not activate, the viewport meta tag has user-scalable=no or maximum-scale=1.
Failure: Pinch-to-zoom blocked. Content cannot be enlarged. WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
Pass: Page zooms freely. Content reflows at zoom.
2
Content breaks at largest font size
1
Settings, Accessibility, Font Size. Drag to the largest setting.
2
Open the site in Chrome. Navigate each key page and interactive component.
3
Check for text overflow, truncation, overlapping, or disappearing elements.
Failure: Text extends beyond container boundaries. Buttons clip labels. Fixed-height containers crop content. Nav items overflow.
Pass: All text readable and all interactive elements accessible at the largest font size.
3
Animations not removed under Remove Animations
1
Settings, Accessibility, Remove Animations, Toggle ON.
2
Open the site in Chrome. Navigate between pages and trigger interactive components.
3
Note any animation still playing at full intensity.
Failure: Page transitions still animate. Carousels auto-advance with motion. Spinners still spin at speed. The site is not respecting prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.
Pass: Animations significantly reduced or removed. All content remains functional.
4
Reflow and zoom check
1
Open the site in Chrome on Android (portrait). Scroll through all key pages at default zoom.
2
Pinch-to-zoom to approximately 200%. Scroll through all pages vertically.
3
Check all navigation, forms, modals, and tables remain accessible and usable.
Pass: No horizontal scroll required. All nav items accessible. Modals openable and closable. Forms completable.
Failure: Horizontal scroll at any zoom level. Items cut off. Interactive components unreachable.

Test on Real Native Apps

These apps let you observe zoom, font scaling, and reduced motion behaviour in a native context where CSS media queries do not apply.

iOS
Appt App
Shows correct and incorrect implementations of font scaling and motion handling in native iOS.
Zoom tests: observe Dynamic Type support vs fixed-size text. Check Reduce Motion behaviour on transitions.
Android
Appt App
Android version with font scale and animation examples.
Zoom tests: observe sp vs dp text sizing. Check Remove Animations system setting.
iOSAndroid
Deque University Mars Demo
Web-based demo with real reflow and zoom failures. Open in mobile Safari or Chrome.
Zoom tests: pinch-to-zoom, horizontal overflow, nav items at zoom, modal behaviour at large text
iOS
Accessibility Techniques
By Paul J. Adam. SwiftUI examples comparing correct Dynamic Type support vs fixed text sizing.
Zoom tests: compare Dynamic Type-compliant vs non-compliant text at largest font setting
