A11y Test Playground: Focus Indicators

Seven scenarios to test with keyboard only. Six are intentionally broken. One passes. Your job is to find the failures — and understand why they fail.

🎯 How to use: unplug your mouse. Press Tab to navigate. Watch what happens to focus. If you cannot see where you are — that is the defect.
1
No focus indicator anywhere
The most common failure. Everything broken at once.
What to do: Tab through the button, link, and input below. WCAG 2.4.7
What to look for: No visible ring on any element. You cannot tell where focus is.
Scenario 1 — outline: none on everything
Read more
Fail WCAG 2.4.7 *:focus { outline: none }
2
Focus ring hidden under sticky header
Ring exists. You cannot see it.
What to do: Tab into the scrollable area. Keep pressing Tab. WCAG 2.4.11
What to look for: The first button scrolls up under the sticky bar. Focus ring is there but hidden behind it.
Scenario 2 — sticky header obscures focus
Sticky navigation bar
Fail WCAG 2.4.11 Ring obscured by z-index stacking
3
Low contrast focus ring
Ring is there. Invisible against the background.
What to do: Tab to the button and the card below. WCAG 1.4.11
What to look for: Ring colour #d0ccc0 on background #f0ede6 — ratio 1.3:1. Far below the 3:1 minimum.
Scenario 3 — low contrast ring on light background
Focusable card
Fail WCAG 1.4.11 Ring: #d0ccc0 on #f0ede6 = 1.3:1
4
Focus ring buried under overlay
An overlay sits above the focused element.
What to do: Tab to the button. Notice the focus ring. Now observe: the overlay has appeared over it. WCAG 2.4.11
What to look for: Button has focus. Ring is completely hidden under the overlay layer.
Scenario 4 — z-index overlay covers focus ring

Cookie notice overlay. The button behind this has focus — but the ring is invisible because this overlay is covering it.

Fail WCAG 2.4.11 Overlay z-index covers ring
5
Focus ring lost in error state
Ring works on default. Disappears on validation failure.
What to do: Tab to the input. Notice the blue ring. Click Submit (or press Enter on the button). Tab back to the input. WCAG 2.4.7
What to look for: In error state, the CSS rule outline: none overrides the focus ring. Ring disappears entirely.
Scenario 5 — error CSS removes focus ring
Enter a valid email address.
Fail WCAG 2.4.7 .error:focus { outline: none }
6
Programmatic focus with no visible ring
JavaScript moves focus. No style defined for the target.
What to do: Tab to the "Open modal" button. Press Enter to open the modal. WCAG 2.4.7
What to look for: The modal opens and JavaScript moves focus to the heading automatically. The heading receives focus, but has no visible ring. You are now inside the modal with no idea which element has focus. Tab once to reach the Close button to see the contrast with a properly styled element.
Scenario 6 — .focus() called, no :focus style on target

Modal heading

Focus was moved here by JavaScript when the modal opened. Look at the heading above — focus is here right now. Can you see a focus ring? No. That is the defect. The heading has outline: none on :focus with no replacement. Now press Tab once — the Close button has a correct ring for comparison.

Fail WCAG 2.4.7 tabindex="-1" with no :focus style
7
Correct focus indicators — the passing example
This is what all of the above should look like.
What to do: Tab through all elements below. WCAG 2.4.7 · 2.4.11 · 1.4.11
What to look for: Clear 3px blue ring with offset. Visible on all elements. Consistent. Never suppressed.
Scenario 7 — :focus-visible with 3:1+ contrast
Link

Focusable card — ring adapts to border-radius
Pass WCAG 2.4.7 · 2.4.11 · 1.4.11 3px #60a5fa, offset 3px, ratio 4.8:1
