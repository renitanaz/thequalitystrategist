Navigation Practice: VoiceOver iOS Gestures, on One Clean Page

This page has no bugs to find. It exists to build muscle memory for VoiceOver's touch gestures themselves: one realistic, fully accessible sample page with a title, several landmarks, a logical heading structure, interactive elements, a form, a table, images (including one decorative), links, and a live region.

🎯 Before you start: turn on VoiceOver (Settings, Accessibility, VoiceOver) and open this page in Safari on a physical iPhone or iPad. Gestures behave differently, or not at all, in the Simulator.
Work through Blog 23's essential VoiceOver gestures, in order
  1. Page title check: Reload this page and listen to the title announcement. It should identify the page meaningfully.
  2. Landmark navigation: Rotate two fingers on the screen to open the rotor, keep rotating to reach Landmarks, then swipe up or down. Count how many distinct, labelled landmarks you pass through below.
  3. Heading structure: Rotor to Headings, then swipe up or down repeatedly. Confirm the levels step down logically with no skips.
  4. Interactive elements: Swipe right to the "Show details" button and the newsletter checkbox. Double-tap each to activate. Listen for label, role, and state.
  5. Forms: Rotor to Form Controls, then swipe up or down through the contact form fields. Listen for label, type, and required state.
  6. Tables: There is no dedicated table rotor category on iOS. Swipe right through the results table cell by cell and listen for row and column position.
  7. Images: Rotor to Images, then swipe up or down. Three images should announce meaningful alt text; the divider graphic should be silently skipped.
  8. Links: Rotor to Links, then swipe up or down. Every link should describe its destination on its own.
  9. Live regions: Swipe to a heading, then double-tap "Send message" to submit the contact form. Listen for the confirmation without moving focus.
  10. Rotor overview: Rotate two fingers for the full structural overview: cycle through Headings, Links, Form Controls, Images, and Landmarks.
