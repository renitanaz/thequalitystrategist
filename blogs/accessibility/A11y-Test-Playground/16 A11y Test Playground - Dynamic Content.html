Dynamic Content Playground

Eight interactive scenarios. Enable NVDA, VoiceOver or TalkBack, move focus away from each demo area, then trigger the update. Listen for what gets announced: and what doesn't.

🎯 Key rule: always move focus to a heading or paragraph BEFORE triggering each scenario. Live regions only prove themselves when focus is elsewhere.

Visually appears. Screen reader hears nothing.

What to look for: The toast slides in visually. NVDA announces nothing because there is no role="status" or aria-live on the container.

3
Filter results update silently
User applies filter. Results change. No announcement.
What to do: Focus on the heading above the filter. Click a filter button. Listen to NVDA. WCAG 4.1.3NVDA

What to look for: Results update visually. No announcement of what changed or how many results remain.

4
Loading spinner: no completion announcement
Loading starts. Loading ends. NVDA hears the start. Never the end.
What to do: Focus elsewhere. Click Load. Wait 3 seconds. Listen to NVDA. WCAG 4.1.3NVDA

What to look for: "Loading..." may be announced (if there is a live region on the spinner). But when loading completes, nothing is announced.
Scenario 4: loading with no completion announcement

Focus here first, then click Load.

Loading...
Content loaded successfully. Here are your results.
NVDA announces: "Loading..." when spinner appears. When content loads, silence: no live region update on completion.
Fail WCAG 4.1.3 Update live region with completion message when load finishes
5
aria-atomic missing: partial announcement only
Cart counter updates. NVDA announces just the number.
What to do: Focus on the paragraph. Click Add. Listen carefully to NVDA. WCAG 4.1.3NVDA

What to look for: NVDA announces "2" when the count changes from 1 to 2: not "2 items in cart." The surrounding context is missing because aria-atomic is not set.
Scenario 5: live region without aria-atomic

Focus here first, then click Add item.

Cart: 0 items

NVDA announces: "1" (or just the updated number): no surrounding context. aria-atomic="true" would announce "Cart: 1 items" as a complete phrase.
Fail WCAG 4.1.3 Add aria-atomic="true" to announce full region text
6
role="alert" used for non-critical update
Interrupts mid-sentence for a low-priority message.
What to do: Start reading any page content with NVDA (Insert+Down Arrow). Click Trigger while NVDA is reading. WCAG 4.1.3NVDA

What to look for: NVDA interrupts mid-sentence to announce a non-critical "tip" message. Should be role="status".
Scenario 6: role="alert" on a tips notification

7
Character counter announcing on every keystroke
Live region is correct. Firing rate is not.
What to do: Enable NVDA. Type in the textarea below. WCAG 4.1.3NVDA

What to look for: NVDA announces the character count on every single keystroke. The screen reader becomes unusable as a typing aid.
Scenario 7: character counter on every keystroke
0 / 200 characters
NVDA announces: "1 / 200 characters", "2 / 200 characters", "3 / 200 characters"... on every keystroke. Should announce only when paused, or at intervals (e.g. every 50 characters).
Fail WCAG 4.1.3 Debounce updates: announce on pause or at intervals
