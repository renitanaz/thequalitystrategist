A11y Test Playground

NVDA Playground

Eleven scenarios with paired failure and pass examples, covering every pattern in Blog 20's "What to Test and What to Listen For." Enable NVDA + Firefox before you start. Each scenario shows what NVDA announces for the failure and what it should announce for the correct implementation.

🎯 Core rule: disconnect your mouse. Navigate with keyboard only. If you cannot complete each task by listening alone: that is a defect. Throughout this page, Insert is the NVDA key (also called the NVDA button): NVDA's default modifier for every shortcut below.
← New to these gestures? Warm up on the Navigation Practice page first: one clean, fully accessible page with no bugs to find, built for Blog 20's Navigation Methodology.
1
Heading structure: visual heading, not semantic
Looks like a heading. NVDA does not know it is one.
What to do: Press H to navigate headings. Try to land on "Our Services." WCAG 1.3.1

What to look for: NVDA skips "Our Services" entirely: it is a div with font-size styling, not an h2.
Scenario 1: visual heading vs semantic heading

Fail: div styled as a heading

About us

We are a quality engineering consultancy.

Our Services

We offer testing strategy, automation, and accessibility auditing.

NVDA with H: "About us, heading level 2": then jumps past "Our Services" to the next semantic heading. "Our Services" is not announced as a heading.

Pass: real h3 element

Our Approach

We offer testing strategy, automation, and accessibility auditing.

NVDA with H: "Our Approach, heading level 3": correctly announced and reachable by heading navigation ✓
Fail Pass WCAG 1.3.1 div styled as heading: not an h2/h3 element

8
Page title: the first thing NVDA announces
Press Insert+T right now, on this actual page.
What to do: Press Insert+T right now, on this page. WCAG 2.4.2

What to look for: This is a genuinely live test: it reads this page's real <title>, not a simulated demo.
Live: this page's own title
NVDA announces: "20 A11y Test Playground - NVDA, The Quality Strategist": the title identifies both the specific page and the site, so a user with several tabs open can tell them apart by title alone ✓

A page title matters most when a screen reader user has several tabs open, or when NVDA announces it on every navigation in a single-page app. A generic title gives no way to tell tabs apart without switching to each one.

What a failure sounds like (for reference: not tested on this page): "Untitled document" or "React App": every tab in the browser announces the same thing, so a screen reader user can't identify which tab holds which page.
Pass WCAG 2.4.2 Every page needs a unique, descriptive title

Expand it. Does NVDA say so?

What to look for: Toggle 1 never tells you it's a button, or whether it's expanded. Toggle 2 announces both, every time.
Scenario 10: aria-expanded present vs missing

Toggle 1: plain div, no role or state

Show details
Extra detail text appears here.
NVDA announces: "Show details" with no role and no state: NVDA never identifies it as a button, and never announces whether the panel is open or closed.

Toggle 2: aria-expanded kept in sync

Extra detail text appears here.
NVDA announces: "Show details, collapsed, button" then, after activating, "Show details, expanded, button": the role and the state are both announced, every time ✓

11
Elements list: structural audit in 30 seconds
Insert+F7: your fastest structural overview tool.
What to do: Press Insert+F7. Switch between Headings, Links, and Form Fields tabs. Review what is listed. Technique

What to look for: Headings tab shows the page structure. Links tab shows all link text: any "read more" or "click here" are immediately visible. Form Fields tab shows all inputs and their labels.
Scenario 11: use the elements list on this page

Press Insert+F7 now. The elements list opens as a dialog. Use it to audit this playground page:

  • Headings tab: is the heading hierarchy logical?
  • Links tab: are all link texts descriptive?
  • Form Fields tab: do all inputs show their labels?
  • Buttons tab: are all button labels meaningful?
Target: every heading announced with a level, every link with a clear destination, every field with a label. This is the 30-second structural audit.
