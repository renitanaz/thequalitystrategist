A11y Test Playground: Keyboard Testing

Five broken scenarios covering steps 1, 2, 4, 5, and 6 of the 6-step keyboard test sequence: tab reach, tab order, activation, component interaction, and keyboard traps. A sixth scenario shows each one fixed, so you can hear and feel what "correct" is too.

🎯 How to use: unplug your mouse. Press Tab, Enter, Space, and Escape. Find what does not work.
👁️ Step 3, focus visibility, has its own dedicated set of scenarios: see the Focus Indicators Playground.

Step 1: Tab reach verification
Clickable with a mouse. Invisible to Tab.
What to do: Tab through this section from the top. WCAG 2.1.1
What to look for: "Add to cart" is a styled <div> with a click handler and no tabindex. Tab skips straight past it to the real button beside it.
Scenario 1: div with onclick, no tabindex
Add to cart
Try clicking each one, then try reaching each one with Tab only.
Fail WCAG 2.1.1 No tabindex, no keyboard handler

Step 2: Tab order validation
Visually first. Reached third.
What to do: Tab through the four fields below and watch the "Tab stop" number light up in each one. WCAG 2.4.3
What to look for: The visual reading order is First name, Last name, Email, Phone. The actual tab order (explicit tabindex values) is Email, First name, Phone, Last name.
Scenario 2: tabindex does not match visual order
Fail WCAG 2.4.3 tabindex order ≠ reading order
3
Step 4: Activation testing
Reachable by Tab. Space does nothing.
What to do: Tab to the checkbox below. Press Space. WCAG 2.1.1
What to look for: This is a <div role="checkbox"> with a click handler and a working tabindex, so it is reachable. But nothing was wired up to respond to a keydown, so Space and Enter do nothing. It only toggles on mouse click.
Scenario 3: role="checkbox" with a click handler only
Email me updates
Try Space or Enter on the checkbox after tabbing to it, then try clicking it.
Fail WCAG 2.1.1 onclick only, no keydown handler

Step 5: Component interaction testing
Expands visually. Silent to screen readers.
What to do: Tab to the accordion trigger below. Press Enter to expand it. WCAG 4.1.2
What to look for: The panel visually opens, but aria-expanded on the trigger never changes from "false". A screen reader user hears no confirmation that anything happened.
Scenario 4: aria-expanded never updates
Orders ship within two business days. International shipping available at checkout.
Fail WCAG 4.1.2 aria-expanded hardcoded to false

Step 6: Keyboard trap verification
No Escape key. No way back.
What to do: Tab to "Open dialog" and press Enter. Once inside, try Tab, Shift+Tab, and Escape to leave. WCAG 2.1.2
What to look for: Focus cycles endlessly between the two elements inside. Escape does nothing. There is no close button reachable by keyboard. This is a Critical defect: log it with the component name, WCAG 2.1.2, and these reproduction steps.
